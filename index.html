Research

What a model learns when we train it

I'm finishing an M2 in Data Science (TRIED) at Université Paris-Saclay, co-accredited with Institut Polytechnique de Paris. Before that I completed a five-year engineering degree and a Master 2 in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at ENSIA in Algiers, where my final-year project received 17.5/20.

With the University of Birmingham, we showed that supervised fine-tuning can teach a language model the format of correct answers without the reasoning behind them, and that reinforcement learning against symbolic verifiers repairs it. I also built LLM world models that predict how a planning environment changes after each action, and evaluated how they generalise to domains they have never seen.

On the engineering side, I led the AI team at Nojoom.AI that trained Arabic language models up to 42 billion parameters, and I care about making training and inference efficient, from data selection down to GPU kernels.
